#Yunomi 

Making sense of your days, one moment at a time.

### Get to know yourself, a little better.

Some days you feel unstoppable. Other days, you're just getting through. Most days fall somewhere in between.

**Yunomi is built for all of them.**

A personal space to check in with yourself, build meaningful habits, and discover the little patterns that shape your everyday life.

No pressure to be perfect. No endless productivity checklists. Just you, your progress, and a better understanding of what makes you feel like yourself.

## The idea

We believe understanding yourself shouldn't feel like another task.

Yunomi brings your moods, habits, daily activities, and wellness patterns together to help you see the bigger picture.

## What we're building

- **Daily Check-ins** — A moment to acknowledge how you're really feeling.
- **Your Rhythm** — Track the habits and routines that matter to you.
- **Connect the Dots** — Discover meaningful patterns between your activities and emotions.
- **A Little Perspective** — Personalized insights and gentle encouragement.
- **Wellness, Connected** — Built with React Native, Expo, and TypeScript for cross-platform compatibility with iOS and Android. Planned integrations include Apple Health and Android Health Connect.
